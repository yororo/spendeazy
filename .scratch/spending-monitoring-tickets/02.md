## What to build

Dashboard distinguishes total, Budgeted, and Unbudgeted Spending and exposes Category risks even when aggregate usage is low; all existing Budget presentations use exact status boundaries.

Scope: parent User Stories 5–9; ID-2.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Use exact cents: below 80% Within Budget; 80% to below 100% Nearing Budget; exactly 100% At Budget Limit; above 100% Over Budget. Round display values only.
- [ ] Apply the policy consistently to Dashboard, existing Insights/Category summaries, and available chart details through a narrowly shared policy for concrete callers.
- [ ] Label the aggregate Budgeted spending / total monthly limits, using only monthly-budgeted Categories in its numerator and current monthly limits in its denominator.
- [ ] Show total recorded spending and Unbudgeted Spending separately; yearly-only Categories and Uncategorized contribute to Unbudgeted Spending.
- [ ] Show individual Category status and exact remaining/over amounts despite low aggregate usage. When there are no monthly limits, show setup guidance rather than an invalid percentage.
- [ ] Test PHP 799.99, 800.00, 999.99, 1000.00, and 1000.01 against a PHP 1000 limit, display rounding traps, yearly-only/no Budgets, and a breach hidden by low aggregate usage. Do not claim empty recorded months are complete or healthy.
