## What to build

After a confirmed import, Users see a focused success message and can browse precisely the saved statement expenses across months before returning to their previous monthly view.

Scope: parent User Stories 32–36; ID-4, ID-7.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Success scrolls to and focuses the announced confirmation heading and shows the destination Space, included expense count, and total.
- [ ] View Transactions passes the destination Space and Committed Statement Import identity; use the existing API statementImportId filter and bypass the browser month clamp in statement scope.
- [ ] Show a visible statement filter/context, recorded expense activity date range, complete filtered count, and total. Do not describe the activity range as complete coverage.
- [ ] Replace the month picker during statement scope; additional filters narrow that scope without including unrelated expenses.
- [ ] Return to monthly view restores the prior Reporting Period; scope changes reset pagination/cache context and provide honest loading/error/empty states.
- [ ] Test a fictional committed statement spanning two months, unrelated same-date expenses, a prior Reporting Period outside that range, extra filters, pagination, and Space ownership. Reconciliation, duplicate protection, and atomic commit remain unchanged.
