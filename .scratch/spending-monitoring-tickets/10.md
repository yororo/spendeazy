## What to build

Users see conservative potential-spending-pattern signals with transparent comparison evidence and contributing Transactions, or an honest insufficient-history state.

Scope: parent User Stories 42, 43; ID-8.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Use up to six preceding calendar months and require at least three positive recorded Category comparisons; do not reach further back to fill missing months.
- [ ] For the current month compare through the equivalent day, clamping shorter months; for a historical selected month use full months. Exclude comparisons with no recorded Category spending and prevent selected/future month leakage.
- [ ] Flag only when the selected amount is at least 1.5 times the median AND at least PHP 500 above it; use exact amounts, including precision for even-sized medians.
- [ ] Show selected amount, baseline window/cutoff, contributing months, median, relative/absolute change, and contributing Transactions with useful investigation actions.
- [ ] Show insufficient recorded history below three eligible comparisons and describe potential patterns in recorded spending; no-signal and empty data are not proof of normal spending or complete coverage.
- [ ] Test eligibility/window boundaries, both noise thresholds and equality, even medians, partial months, shorter months/leap years, future leakage, sparse data, and visible explanation/actions. Do not add affordability judgments or forecasts.
