## What to build

Users can assess recurring Category breaches, review a current recurring Budget, and understand that changing it affects historical comparisons.

Scope: parent User Stories 44, 45; ID-3, ID-8.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Suggest reviewing a monthly Budget after breaches in at least three of the last six eligible recorded Category months.
- [ ] Use completed months at/before a historical selected period or before the current partial month; exclude future periods and months without recorded Category spending. Show actual eligible denominator and current-limit evidence.
- [ ] Open the Category's existing Budget editor with evidence available; Unbudgeted Categories offer Set Budget. The User enters and explicitly saves any new amount; never auto-adjust limits.
- [ ] Before saving, explain historical comparisons will use the new current recurring limit; label historical views accordingly and refresh dependent queries after success.
- [ ] Preserve yearly Budgets, current positive monthly limits, existing stale-write protection, Space authorization, and cancellation/return context; add no effective-dated Budget history.
- [ ] Test recurring breach eligibility/counts, exact-limit non-breach, historical/current cutoffs, no-monthly-Budget actions, save/cancel/stale failure, and recalculated historical evidence. This slice is independent of unusual-spending median signals.
